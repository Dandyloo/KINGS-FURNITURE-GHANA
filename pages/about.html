Who we are

Three decades of craft.
One uncompromising standard.

Kings Furniture Ghana was founded in Takoradi in 1992 with a single purpose — to build furniture that lasts. Not just in durability, but in design. Pieces that earn their place in a room and hold it for generations.

Today we operate two showrooms across Ghana, employ over 120 skilled artisans, and have furnished more than 1,000 homes, offices and institutions. Every piece still carries the same standard we set on day one.

Our journey

Milestones that
shaped us.

1992

Founded in Takoradi

Kings Furniture opens its first workshop in Market Circle, Takoradi with a team of 12 craftsmen and a vision to build Ghana's finest furniture.

2000

First showroom opens

We open our first dedicated showroom in Takoradi, allowing customers to experience our full range of furniture in person.

2008

Expanding to Accra

Growing demand from Accra leads to our second showroom, bringing Kings Furniture to the capital city.

2015

Commercial projects division

We launch a dedicated commercial projects team serving hotels, offices and institutions across Ghana.

2024

Over 1,000 homes furnished

We pass the milestone of 1,000 completed home fit-outs, cementing our position as Ghana's most trusted furniture brand.

What drives us

The values behind
every piece.

01

Craft over convenience

We never cut corners. Every joint is hand-fitted, every surface hand-finished. It takes longer — and it lasts longer.

02

Materials that matter

We source only the finest hardwoods and fabrics. If it won't last a generation, it doesn't leave our workshop.

03

Built for real life

Beautiful furniture that can't withstand daily use isn't beautiful at all. We design for how people actually live.

04

Proudly Ghanaian

Every piece is designed, crafted and finished in Ghana by Ghanaian hands. We don't import what we can build better here.
